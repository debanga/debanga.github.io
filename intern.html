Instructions for Prospective Interns

Requirement Details
How to apply
  • Method 1: MFSDS&AI Internship Form. You can find the form in our website when it is available. Please do not email me asking any further details regarding the form, you can simply visit and check if it is available.
  • Method 2: Sending email request to Grp_pmal@iitg.ac.in with your CV. We have a rigourous selection process and acceptance rate is very low.
Accommodation
  • On-campus accommodation is possible only for students applying through the MFSDS&AI internship form.
  • For all other internships, no campus accommodation can be provided. Online internship is allowed.
Internships
  • A confirmation from PI (Dr. Debanga Raj Neog) is required.
  • After confirmation, send an email to dneog@iitg.ac.in confirming that there are no parallel internships. The email should include:
    • Updated CV
    • NOC from your institute (in official letter head) clearly mentioning MFSDS&AI name, stating the nature (online/offline) of the internship, and confirming that this will be the only internship.
    • Photocopy of your institute ID card.
  • Unless a custom project work is assigned, by default, it is expected that the intern will select a CVPR/ICCV/ECCV/MICCAI/AAAI or any CORE A conference paper published in last two years and implement from scratch. It is the intern's responsibility to select a relevant paper and propose it to me for approval.
  • After completing the internship (including the implementation of the above and any novel improvements, if applicable), submit a project completion report of at least 20 pages. A template is available here. Submit your final report here.

    • The certificate will be issued only after fulfilling the above requirements. Any violation of the “no parallel internship” policy will result in non-issuance or cancellation of the certificate.

Letter of Recommendation (LOR)
  • At least one submission (in review is fine) to a conference/journal is mandatory.
  • Maximum of 3 LORs (strictly for higher studies) per student will be issued, so please plan ahead.
  • Advance intimation of one month is necessary for an LOR request; otherwise, the request will not be entertained.
