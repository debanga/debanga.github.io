Instructions for Prospective Interns

Requirement Details
How to apply
  • Method 1: MFSDS&AI Internship Form. You can find the form in our website when it is available. Please do not email me asking any further details regarding the form, you can simply visit and check if it is available.
  • Method 2: Sending email request to Grp_pmal@iitg.ac.in. We have a rigourous selection process and acceptance rate is very low.
Accommodation
  • On-campus accommodation is possible only for students applying through the MFSDS&AI internship form.
  • For all other internships, no campus accommodation can be provided. Online internship is allowed.
Internships
  • A confirmation from PI (Dr. Debanga Raj Neog) is required.
  • After confirmation, send an email to dneog@iitg.ac.in confirming that there are no parallel internships. The email should include:
    • NOC from your institute clearing mentioning MFSDS&AI name, stating the nature (online/offline) of the internship, and confirming that this will be the only internship.
    • Photocopy of your institute ID card.
  • After completion of the internship, send a minimum 20-page project completion report. Template will be provided on request.

    • Only after satisfying the above, the certificate will be issued. Violation of the "no parallel internship" criteria will result in non-issuance or cancellation of the certificate.
Letter of Recommendation (LOR)
  • At least one submission (in review is fine) to a conference/journal is mandatory.
  • Maximum of 5 LORs (strictly for higher studies) per student will be issued, so please plan ahead.
  • Advance intimation of one month is necessary for an LOR request; otherwise, the request will not be entertained.
